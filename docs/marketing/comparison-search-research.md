# Comparison search research

Research date: **4 October 2026**. Scope: all existing comparison detail pages and the comparison hub.

## Method and limits

These are ten **prioritised search opportunities per page**, not a measured top-ten popularity ranking. No monthly search volumes, paid keyword dataset, difficulty scores or traffic forecasts were obtained. Priorities favour relevant competitor/category intent and observed search coverage, not fabricated numbers.

- **A — autocomplete evidence:** the query or a close topic/wording variant appeared in live English Google predictions. Later batches requested US results. **High** means stronger observed relevance, not high measured search volume.
- **S — search-result evidence:** a matching topic surfaced in live results. **Medium** normally means a useful comparison intent with weaker direct demand evidence. Synara’s directory evidence is explicitly **Low**.
- **I — inferred:** an editorial long-tail question derived from buyer needs or adjacent evidence. **Low/exploratory** means demand is not established. Official feature documentation alone is not keyword-demand evidence.
- Exact candidate wording is not proven search volume, even for an observed topic. Predictions vary with language, location and other factors; absent predictions do not prove absent searches. See [Google’s explanation](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/7368877?hl=en).
- Live result pages, especially vendor comparisons and generated directories, are discovery evidence only. Product claims use official sources actually opened; each detail page records those links and its own source-review date. No third-party ranking was treated as a benchmark.
- Every target maps to a visible heading, table row or FAQ. Related queries share an answer when that is more useful than keyword stuffing. The content does not promise a ranked “best ten” list it does not contain.

All linked autocomplete seeds were rechecked in English/US on 4 October 2026. Selected actual predictions are recorded below, so the demand evidence does not depend on an ephemeral scratch file. Earlier search results supply complementary topic evidence. Predictions and results may change after this date.

## Editorial decisions

Use plain product names, answer the choice early, and preserve genuine competitor advantages. Distinguish an agent from its workspace; free software from model charges and hosted compute; tool execution from model inference; local storage from privacy. Do not claim exclusive parallelism, schedules, remote access or local models. Keep publication dates separate from source-review dates. FAQs use the same strings in visible HTML and structured data; no search-result enhancement is promised.
